SAHYOG

( sahyog image )

SAHYOG – Sharing Smiles supports the holistic development of underprivileged children through education and consistent mentoring.

  • Daily teaching sessions from 3:00 PM to 5:00 PM.
  • Annual cultural programs and creative workshops.
  • Providing books, bags, and essential school supplies.
  • Bridge courses to bring dropout children back to mainstream schools.
