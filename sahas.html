SAHAS

[ Sahas Empowerment Image ]

SAHAS – Empowering Women focuses on creating an inclusive environment where women are equipped with skills to lead independent lives.

  • Vocational training: Stitching, handicrafts, and computer literacy.
  • Awareness sessions on health, hygiene, and reproductive rights.
  • Legal aid workshops regarding domestic rights and safety.
  • Self-defense camps and leadership development meets.
